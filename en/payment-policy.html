Payment Policy

Last Updated: 24 September 2026

We aim to keep our payment process simple and transparent.

1. Service Price

The price displayed for a listed service is the standard final price for that service within its stated scope.

If a customer's requirement falls outside the listed scope, any additional cost will be communicated and confirmed before the additional work begins.

2. Free Short Draft

For paid/custom projects, a short draft/sample (1–2 sample drafts) is provided before the 50% advance so that the customer can understand the proposed direction of the work.

The free draft is a short sample (1–2 sample drafts), not the complete final deliverable. Separately, the first eligible new-customer service up to ₹500 may be free, including the draft and final delivery, subject to the offer terms shown on the website.

Sample Privacy: Any dummy information (such as mobile numbers or brand logos) shown in the free sample/draft is for demonstration only; real information is used only after the project is confirmed and the 50% advance is received, except where the separate free-service offer applies.

3. Payment & Delivery — 50% / 50% Flow

For paid/custom projects, after the free consultation/sample is reviewed and the project is confirmed, work begins after a 50% advance payment.

A working preview is shared during the process for review and agreed revisions, preferably with a watermark or other reasonable protection.

The remaining 50% payment is due after the working preview is approved and agreed revisions are completed, and before final delivery.

4. Final Delivery After Balance Payment

The final full-resolution or agreed deliverable is handed over only after the remaining 50% balance payment is confirmed.

5. Payment Confirmation

The customer should retain the payment confirmation or transaction record until the service has been completed.

6. Additional Work

No additional paid work will be undertaken without informing the customer of the applicable additional charge and obtaining confirmation.

7. Failed or Unconfirmed Payments

A service will not be treated as paid until the payment has been successfully received or otherwise confirmed.

If a payment is pending, failed or reversed, final delivery may be placed on hold until the payment issue is resolved.

8. Payment and Refund Records

Relevant order, payment and communication records may be retained where reasonably necessary for service delivery, customer support, accounting, dispute handling or legal compliance, subject to our Privacy Policy.
