Revision Policy

Last Updated: 24 September 2026

We provide revision support so that the final deliverable can reasonably match the agreed requirement.

1. Seven-Day Revision Support

After final delivery, customers can request revisions within 7 days of delivery.

During this period, applicable revisions are provided without an additional revision charge.

2. What Counts as a Revision?

A revision means a reasonable modification to the delivered work based on the originally agreed requirement. Examples may include:

  • correcting information supplied by the customer;
  • adjusting wording;
  • making reasonable design changes;
  • correcting formatting; or
  • making other changes that keep the work within the original scope.

3. New Requirements

A completely new concept, substantially different direction, additional quantity, additional functionality or requirement outside the original scope may be treated as a new service or additional work.

In such cases, the applicable price will be communicated before the additional work begins.

4. Revisions After 7 Days

After the 7-day revision period, revision charges may apply:

Service tierRevision charge
₹1,000 services₹50–₹100 per revision
₹500 services₹20–₹50 per revision
₹100 services₹20 per revision
₹50 services (single-item orders)₹10 per revision

The exact charge will depend on the nature of the requested change and will be communicated before the revision is carried out.

5. AI-Generated Output

Because AI-generated results can vary, the final output may differ from an initial draft or expectation in certain visual, textual, audio or video characteristics.

We will make reasonable efforts to adjust the output according to the agreed requirement, subject to the capabilities and limitations of the tools used.

6. Revision Requests

Revision requests should be communicated clearly through WhatsApp or Gmail so that the requested changes can be properly understood and recorded.
