Refund Policy

Last Updated: 24 September 2026

We aim to provide clear, useful and requirement-based digital services. Because our services may involve customized digital work, AI-generated content and other digital deliverables, our refund process is based on the nature and status of the work.

1. Review Before Final Delivery

A short draft/sample (1–2 sample drafts) is provided before final delivery so that you can review the general direction of the work. Any dummy information (such as mobile numbers or brand logos) shown in this sample is for demonstration only; real information is used only after the project is confirmed and the 50% advance is received, except where the separate free-service offer applies.

You should communicate any important changes or concerns before final work is completed.

2. Customized Digital Work

Once a customized service has been completed and the agreed final deliverable has been provided, a refund is not automatically available simply because the customer changes their preference, changes their mind, or no longer wants the completed work.

Where appropriate, we will first try to resolve genuine issues through the applicable revision process.

3. When a Refund May Be Considered

A refund request may be reviewed where, for example:

  • the delivered work materially fails to match the agreed requirement;
  • the agreed service cannot reasonably be completed;
  • there is a significant issue attributable to the service provider that cannot reasonably be corrected through revision; or
  • a refund is otherwise required under applicable law.

Any approved refund will be handled according to the circumstances of the order and applicable law.

4. Refund Request Period

If you believe there is a genuine issue with the delivered service, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and clearly explain the issue.

The 7-day period is intended to provide a reasonable review and revision window; it does not create an automatic right to a refund.

5. Customer Preference or Change of Mind

A refund is generally not provided merely because:

  • the customer changes their mind after approving the work;
  • the customer's preference changes after the work has been completed;
  • the customer no longer needs the service;
  • the customer requests a substantially different project from the originally agreed requirement; or
  • the delivered work meets the agreed requirement but the customer subsequently prefers a different style or direction.

Where possible, such changes may be handled as a revision or a new requirement.

6. Digital Deliverables

Digital services and deliverables cannot generally be physically returned. If a refund is approved, we may request that the customer stop using, distributing or commercially exploiting the refunded deliverable and delete copies where reasonably applicable.

7. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, the applicable refund amount and payment method will be communicated to the customer.

Refund processing may depend on the payment method or payment provider used.

8. No Waiver of Legal Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive any consumer right or remedy that cannot legally be excluded or restricted under applicable law.

For questions regarding a refund, please contact us through the communication method provided on the website.

This is website copy, not formal legal advice or a guarantee of legal protection.
