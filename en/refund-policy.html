Refund Policy

How much is refunded

  • If you cancel before the project is finished, whether within 24 hours of the project start or later, you get back the full amount you have paid. The project starts when you pay the 50% advance, after seeing the free preview drafts.
  • If you cancel after the project is finished (the work is complete and 100% payment has been received), 25% to 50% of the payment is returned. The exact percentage within this range is decided according to the work done and the time spent on it.
  • A refund can be requested up to 7 days after delivery. After 7 days there is no refund.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Send me a message on WhatsApp saying “refund”.
  2. I will send you a refund agreement.
  3. Through the agreement, you confirm that you will immediately remove the delivered work from social media and stop using it, and that your right to use it ends with the refund.
  4. Once I receive the signed agreement, the refund is processed within 7 days.

This is a private agreement prepared by me. It is not a government document.
