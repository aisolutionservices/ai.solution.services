Refund at a glance

When you cancel or askRefund
Before the work is completed: If you cancel at any time before the work is completed, you receive a full refund of the amount paid. The project begins when you pay the 50% advance after reviewing the free preview drafts.Full refund
After completion, before delivery: If you cancel after the work is completed (100% payment received) and before it is delivered, 50% of the payment is refunded.50% refund

Example for a ₹500 service: you pay a ₹250 advance to start. If you cancel before the work is completed, you get ₹250 back. Once you have paid the remaining ₹250 and the work is completed, you get ₹250 back (50% of ₹500) if you cancel before delivery or ask within 7 days of delivery. After 7 days from delivery, there is no refund.

  4. Once I receive the signed agreement, the refund is processed within 7 days.
