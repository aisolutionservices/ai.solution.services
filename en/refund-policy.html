Refund Policy

Refund amount

  • Before project completion: If you cancel before the work is completed, whether within 24 hours of the project start or later, you receive a full refund of the amount paid. The project begins when you pay the 50% advance after reviewing the free preview drafts.
  • After project completion and before delivery: If you cancel after the work is completed (100% payment received) and before it is delivered, 25% to 50% of the payment is refunded. The exact percentage within this range is determined by the work completed and the time invested.
  • After delivery: Refund requests are accepted up to 7 days after delivery, and 50% of the payment is refunded. No refund is available after 7 days.

How to request a refund

  1. Message me on WhatsApp with the word “refund”.
  2. I will send you a refund agreement.
  3. By signing the agreement, you confirm that you will promptly remove the delivered work from social media and cease all use of it, and that your right to use the work ends upon refund.
  4. Once I receive the signed agreement, the refund is processed within 7 days.
