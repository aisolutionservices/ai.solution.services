Frequently Asked Questions

When is payment required?

For paid/custom projects, the flow is: free consultation/sample → 50% advance after concept approval → work + working preview → agreed revisions → remaining 50% balance → final delivery. The first eligible new-customer service priced up to ₹500 is free under the separate website offer, subject to its stated eligibility and terms; those offer terms apply specifically to that service.

What if I am not sure exactly what I need?

That is fine. Tell me about your situation, and I will help understand the requirement and identify a suitable solution.

What if my requirement is outside the listed service scope?

If that happens, any additional cost or applicable changes will be explained before work begins. No additional charge will be added without your confirmation.

What are the revision charges?

For 7 days after delivery, unlimited revisions within the agreed requirement are free. After 7 days — for ₹1,000 services ₹50–₹100 per revision, ₹500 services ₹20–₹50 per revision, and for the ₹100 service ₹20 per revision. Full details Revision Policy are available there.

Are the listed prices (₹50 / ₹100 / ₹500 / ₹1,000) final?

Yes. The listed price is final for the standard scope. If your requirement is outside the listed scope, any additional cost is confirmed before work begins.

How long does delivery take, and when does the time start?

Standard work is generally delivered within 24–72 hours. The timeframe starts once your requirement has been understood and confirmed. Complex or larger projects may take longer, and you will be informed in advance.

Customized digital work is not automatically refundable after delivery. If the delivered work does not materially match the agreed requirement, an opportunity for revision/correction is provided first; if there is a genuine issue, a refund request may be reviewed within 7 days. Full conditions are available in the Refund Policy.
