Free sample and drafts

Who gets a free sample?

New clients, one time only, on services up to ₹500. The sample is of quantity 1 (for example, 1 image or 1 poster), made with your information and without a watermark. All services also get 1 to 2 free preview drafts before you pay.

What is the difference between a free sample and free preview drafts?

The free sample is one time, for new clients on services up to ₹500: quantity 1, made with your information, without a watermark. Free preview drafts are for every order of any service: 1 to 2 drafts with dummy information and a watermark.

What happens after the free sample?

If you like it, we continue on WhatsApp with the steps on the Process page.

Can I use the free sample or a draft?

No. The free sample, preview drafts and watermarked drafts are for review only. You may use the work after the final delivery.

What are the free preview drafts?

For all services, including those above ₹500, you get 1 to 2 free preview drafts with dummy information and a watermark, so you can see the style before you pay.

What happens after I pay the 50% advance?

Work starts. Within 48 hours of receiving your 50% advance and all your details, you get 1 to 2 free work drafts with your own information and a watermark.

Payment and delivery

50% before work starts, and the remaining 50% after you have seen the work draft and before the watermark is removed and the final work is delivered. Prices are listed in rupees on the Services page.

How can I pay?

By UPI, using any UPI app. Please confirm the amount on WhatsApp before you pay.

When do I get the final work?

After you pay the remaining 50%, the watermark is removed and the final work is delivered within 24 hours.

Revisions
