Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how A.I. Solution Services handles information shared for providing its AI-assisted digital services.

1. Information You May Provide

Depending on the service, you may provide your name, contact details, business information, text, images, documents, logos, references, requirements, payment confirmation and other information needed to complete the requested work.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to understand your requirement, communicate with you, prepare or deliver the requested work, process payment-related confirmation, provide support and handle reasonable service records.

3. Third-Party Tools

Some work may require third-party tools or services, including AI tools, email services, messaging services or payment providers. Information necessary for the requested task may be processed by those services according to their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Free Samples and Dummy Information

Before a paid/custom project is confirmed and the required advance payment is received, free samples/demos use dummy or placeholder information where applicable. Real customer-specific mobile numbers, names, brand logos and similar information are not intentionally used in the free sample stage.

5. Sharing and Sale of Information

Customer information is not intentionally sold to people or companies. Information may be shared with a third-party tool or service only where reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, process a transaction or perform a customer-requested action.

6. Storage and Deletion

Necessary information may be retained for service delivery, customer support, accounting, dispute handling or legal compliance. Unnecessary files are intended to be deleted after a reasonable period where they remain within my control. Deletion may be affected by backups, third-party systems or technical limitations.

7. Security and Confidentiality

Reasonable steps are taken to protect customer information and maintain confidentiality. However, no online service or third-party technology can guarantee absolute security.

8. Customer Responsibility

Please share only information that is necessary and appropriate for the requested service. Do not provide passwords, authentication codes or highly sensitive information unless it is specifically required and appropriate for the service.

9. Your Questions

If you have a privacy-related question or want to clarify how particular information will be handled, please contact me through the WhatsApp or Gmail details provided on the website.

This is website information, not formal legal advice or a guarantee of legal protection.
