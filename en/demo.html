AI problem solving samples

AI-based solutions for sample problems. This is not professional medical or legal advice.

I keep postponing my study even after making a timetable.

Solution steps

  1. Cut the first task down to just 20 minutes, so it is easy to start.
  2. Study at the same time and in the same place every day.
  3. Keep your phone in another room while studying.
  4. Tick each finished task in a notebook.
  5. After 7 days, increase the study time slowly.
My phone storage is always full.

Solution steps

  1. Open Settings and check Storage to see what takes the most space.
  2. Delete big videos and old WhatsApp media.
  3. Back up photos to Google Photos or a computer, then remove them from the phone.
  4. Clear the cache of your apps.
  5. Uninstall apps that you do not use.
I cannot balance my college and my side work.

Solution steps

  1. List all your weekly commitments.
  2. Set separate fixed hours for college and side work.
  3. Every night, write the 3 most important tasks for the next day.
  4. Keep rest time in your calendar too.
  5. Every Sunday, check what worked and what to change.

AI business solving samples

AI-based ideas and steps for sample business problems. This is not professional advice, so please re-check important business decisions with a relevant expert.

A cafe has fewer customers on weekdays.

Solution steps

  1. Offer a weekday combo, such as tea with a snack.
  2. Share the offer on WhatsApp status and in nearby office groups.
  3. Give a small discount between 3 pm and 5 pm.
  4. Collect customer numbers to send repeat offers.
  5. Track weekday sales for 2 weeks and keep what works.
Customers stop replying after I send the price.

Solution steps

  1. Send the price along with 2 short benefits, not just the number.
  2. Add a sample or a result from a past client.
  3. Give a clear next step, such as “Reply YES to start.”
  4. Follow up politely, once, after one day.
  5. Offer a small starter option.
Customers keep asking the same questions.

Solution steps

  1. List the 10 most common questions.
  2. Write short answers and save them as WhatsApp quick replies.
  3. Add the same answers to an FAQ page.
  4. Review the list every month.
