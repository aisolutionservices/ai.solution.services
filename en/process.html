How the Process Works

Our process is designed to be simple, transparent and customer-friendly. Before work begins, you will know what you will receive, the expected timeframe and when payment is due.

→ → → → → → Free Consultation / Demo→50% Advance→Work + Preview→Revisions→50% Balance→Final Delivery
  1. 01

    Free Consultation / Requirement

    Discuss your requirement through WhatsApp or Gmail. We first understand what you need and whether the work fits the selected service or custom scope.

  2. 02

    Free Sample / Concept

    A limited 1–2 sample concept is shown for review. Where applicable, the sample is dummy or watermarked: mobile numbers, brand logos, names and other customer-specific information are not real at this stage.

  3. 03

    50% Advance Payment

    After you approve the concept and confirm the project, the applicable 50% advance payment is received before actual project work starts. The free sample itself does not use real customer information.

  4. 04

    Work + Working Preview

    Actual project work begins after the advance. A working preview is shared, preferably watermarked or otherwise protected, so you can review the real project direction and progress.

  5. 05

    Revisions

    Reasonable revisions within the agreed scope are handled on the working preview. Additional requirements or a substantially different direction may be treated as additional work.

  6. 06

    50% Balance Payment

    Once the working preview is approved and agreed revisions are completed, the remaining 50% balance is due before final delivery.

  7. 07

    Final Delivery + 7-Day Support

    After the balance payment is confirmed, the full-resolution/final agreed deliverables are handed over. Standard delivery is generally 24–72 hours depending on the work. Applicable revision support remains available for 7 days after final delivery.

Refund Policy: If the delivered work does not materially match the agreed requirement within 7 days of delivery, a refund request may be reviewed — full details are available in the Refund Policy.

Custom Projects: For work outside the listed service scope, a 50% advance payment is required before work begins, with the remaining 50% due before final delivery — see the Payment Policy for details.

Privacy & Confidentiality: Information and files you share are handled confidentially as described in the Privacy Policy and are used for providing the requested service.

Sample Privacy: Any free sample/demo shown before payment uses dummy information where applicable. Real customer information is used only after the project is confirmed and the required advance payment is received.

Custom Projects: For custom projects, the final flow is Free Consultation / Demo → 50% Advance → Work + Preview → Revisions → 50% Balance → Final Delivery.
