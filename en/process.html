Process

From your first message to a confirmed order

  1. Choose a service or package on the Services page.
  2. Send me your requirement on WhatsApp or by email: the service, what you need, the quantity and your deadline.
  3. We confirm the service, the price and the details together.
  4. New clients can get 1 free sample. Every order gets 1 to 2 free preview drafts before you pay.
  5. You pay the 50% advance. Your order is confirmed when I receive this advance, and the work starts.

Your order, step by step

  1. Free sample (new clients)
  2. Free preview (every order)
  3. 50% payment
  4. Work draft
  5. 50% payment
  6. Final delivery

The free sample and free drafts are for review only, so you can decide before you commit. Only the paid service gives you the final work you can use.

For new clients (one time)

New clients onlyOne time onlyServices up to ₹500Quantity 1

  1. Get 1 free sample (services up to ₹500). It is made with your information and has no watermark.
  2. If you like it, continue on WhatsApp.

For every order of any service

  1. Get 1 to 2 free preview drafts (all services) with dummy information and a watermark.
  2. Pay 50% in advance. Work starts after this payment.
  3. Within 48 hours of your 50% advance and all your details, get 1 to 2 free work drafts with your information and a watermark.
  4. Pay the remaining 50%.
  5. The watermark is removed and the final work is delivered to you within 24 hours of receiving your remaining 50%.
  6. After payment and final delivery, you get an invoice as a PDF on WhatsApp or email, whichever you choose.
  7. After delivery, you get 9 free revisions within 3 days (3 per day).
Refund in short
  • Before the work is completed: full refund of the amount paid.
  • After completion and before delivery: 50% of the payment is refunded.
  • Within 7 days after delivery: 50% of the payment is refunded. After 7 days: no refund.
  • To ask for a refund, message me on WhatsApp with the word “refund”. I will send you a refund agreement. By signing it, you confirm that you will remove the delivered work from social media and stop using it. The refund is processed within 7 days of receiving the signed agreement.
